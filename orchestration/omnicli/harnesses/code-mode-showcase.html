ModeFilesystemNetworkTool calls

strict (default)home masked; workspace + bindings onlynoneone UDS bridge call to parent; allowlisted verbs only

In strict the plan cannot read ~/.ssh, cannot open a socket, and $`…` throws before spawning. exec --json reports bridge_calls and bridge_denied, so a refused verb is evidence rather than silence.
