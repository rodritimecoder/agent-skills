# One sentence naming the fact

What happened, what it cost, and what a future session should do differently.
Quote the source line; do not paraphrase a decision into a different one.

## Why

The reason, in one line. A rule without a why cannot be re-evaluated when the
environment changes.

## Evidence

The command, error, or file that proves it. The dreamer checks these against
the transcript; an unsupported claim stays `verified: false`.
